# Synastry Basics: Charts in Relationship

**Synastry** overlays two birth charts to see **inter-aspects**: how Person A’s planets land in Person B’s houses, and vice versa.

**Highlights to notice:**
- **Sun–Moon** contacts for basic rapport  
- **Venus–Mars** for chemistry and courtship rhythm  
- **Moon–Moon** for domestic empathy  
- **Saturn** contacts for durability—and sometimes heaviness  
- **Uranus** for spark or instability

**Double whammy** (mutual receptions or tight two-way aspects) intensify the story.

**Caution:** A “good” synastry chart doesn’t replace communication; hard aspects can **mature** relationships with effort.

**Composite charts** differ: they midpoint two charts into one “relationship entity”—useful for group identity of the couple.
