# The Lunar Nodes: North and South

The **lunar nodes** are where the Moon’s orbit crosses the ecliptic. They move backward through the zodiac and are interpreted as **axis of growth (North Node)** and **familiar habit (South Node)**.

**North Node** — experiential stretch: qualities to develop, sometimes uncomfortable at first.  
**South Node** — innate gifts and old patterns; comfort zone that can become a rut if overused.

**House placement** shows *where* in life the story unfolds; **sign placement** shows *how*. Example: North Node in **Gemini** might emphasize curiosity, questions, and diverse skills while releasing Sagittarius-flavored certainty as a default armor (if South Node is Sagittarius—nodes are always opposite).

**Modern caveat:** Not every astrologer uses the same **true vs. mean node** calculation; minor degree differences may shift sign if near a boundary.

**Counseling tone:** Frame the North Node as **invitation**, not homework from the universe.
