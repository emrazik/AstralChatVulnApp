# Lunar Phases and Symbolic Timing

The **lunar cycle** spans roughly 29.5 days from New Moon to New Moon. Many astrologers align intentions with phase **quality**, not magic guarantees.

- **New Moon** — seeding ideas, quiet beginnings, setting aims.  
- **Waxing crescent** — commitment, first visible effort.  
- **First quarter** — decisions, obstacles that clarify direction.  
- **Waxing gibbous** — refinement, adjustment toward a goal.  
- **Full Moon** — culmination, visibility, emotional peak; what began at the prior New Moon often surfaces.  
- **Waning gibbous** — gratitude, sharing lessons, distribution.  
- **Last quarter** — release, cutting clutter, revising plans.  
- **Balsamic / dark Moon** — rest, closure, intuition; low external push.

**Sign of the Moon** adds flavor: a Full Moon in **Scorpio** may intensify secrets and transformation; in **Gemini**, conversation and perspective.

**Practical framing:** Invite users to journal or rest at Full Moons if overwhelmed; suggest gentle planning at New Moons without superstition.
