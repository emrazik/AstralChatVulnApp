# Electional Astrology: Choosing a Moment to Begin

**Electional astrology** picks a **start time** for an action—launching a business, signing, weddings, publishing—hoping the sky supports the intent.

**Principles:** Strengthen **benefics** for the topic (Venus for art, **Jupiter** for growth), avoid **Mercury retrograde** if clarity matters (not a universal rule), consider **Moon void-of-course** traditions, and respect client constraints (Saturday only? winter only?).

**Realism:** Perfect charts rarely exist; astrologers optimize within windows and client ethics.

**Transparency:** Disclose that outcomes depend on many factors; astrology aids **timing psychology** and intention, not guarantees.

**Contrast:** **Horary** answers a specific question from a chart cast for the moment the astrologer understands the question—different branch than electional.
