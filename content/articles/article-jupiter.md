# Jupiter: Growth, Faith, and Excess

**Jupiter** expands whatever it touches: **beliefs, travel, teaching, luck—but also inflation**. Its sign shows how you seek meaning; its house shows where opportunities come knocking.

**Jupiter return** (~every 12 years) often marks **new chapters** in worldview—study abroad, spiritual seeking, career leaps, or legal themes.

**Hard aspects** to Jupiter can mean overpromising, weight gain, or preachiness; **supportive aspects** bring generosity and mentors.

**Guidance:** Invite users to ask, “Where am I overcommitting?” when Jupiter transits hit natal planets—growth needs **grounding** (Saturn’s lesson) to last.
