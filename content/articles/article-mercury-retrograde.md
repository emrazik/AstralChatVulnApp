# Mercury Retrograde: What It Is (and Isn’t)

**Mercury retrograde** is an apparent backward motion of Mercury from Earth’s viewpoint. It happens several times a year for about three weeks. Astrologically, Mercury governs **perception, language, logistics, contracts, devices, and short trips**.

**Common themes:** revisiting old conversations, delays, typos, rescheduled plans, rediscovering lost files, reconnecting with people from the past. It’s a cycle that favors **review** over **launch**.

**Myths:** Mercury retrograde is not a universal curse. Many people sign leases, ship products, and marry during retrogrades without doom. The skill is **double-checking details**, backing up data, and leaving buffer time.

**Shadow and stations:** Some astrologers track the **shadow** period when Mercury crosses degrees it will revisit. **Stations** (when Mercury appears to change direction) can feel extra conspicuous for Mercury-ruled tasks.

**Tone for users:** Normalize inconveniences without fear; suggest reflection, edits, and patience rather than paralysis.
