# Modalities: Cardinal, Fixed, and Mutable

Each element contains three signs distinguished by **modality**—how they initiate and sustain change.

**Cardinal (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn)** — starters, leaders, pivots; jump first, may burn out if overwhelmed.  
**Fixed (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)** — stabilizers, stubborn depth, loyalty; resist change until ready, then unshakeable.  
**Mutable (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces)** — adapters, editors, translators between seasons; flexible, sometimes scattered.

**Compatibility:** Cardinals spark friction if all want to lead; mutables may need clearer agreements; fixed pairings endure if compromise exists.

**Workplace lens:** Cardinals propose, fixeds maintain standards, mutables handle shifting specs. Naming modality reduces personal blame—“we’re wired differently on pacing.”
