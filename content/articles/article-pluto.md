# Pluto: Power, Transformation, and Letting Go

**Pluto** represents **depth, power, taboo, regeneration**, and what must **die to be reborn**. It moves slowly; sign and house placements mark generational and personal **underworld** work.

**Pluto transits** (especially to Sun, Moon, Ascendant, or angles) can coincide with **identity crisis**, loss, obsession, therapy breakthroughs, or reclaiming agency.

**Shadow:** Control, secrecy, all-or-nothing bonds. **Gold:** emotional honesty and resilient vulnerability.

**Celeste note:** Normalize grief cycles during Pluto activations; empower choice and consent rather than catastrophizing fate.
