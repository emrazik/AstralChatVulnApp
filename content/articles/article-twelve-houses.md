# The Twelve Astrological Houses (Overview)

Houses are **segments of the sky** relative to your birth time and place. Each links life arenas to planetary activity.

| House | Traditional themes |
|------|---------------------|
| 1st | Self, body, first impressions, approach to life |
| 2nd | Money, values, possessions, self-worth |
| 3rd | Siblings, neighbors, short trips, learning, messaging |
| 4th | Home, parents, roots, private emotional base |
| 5th | Romance, play, children, creative projects |
| 6th | Routines, health habits, service, coworkers |
| 7th | Partnerships, marriage, open enemies, contracts |
| 8th | Shared resources, debt, intimacy, transformation |
| 9th | Travel, higher education, beliefs, publishing |
| 10th | Career, reputation, public role, authority |
| 11th | Friends, groups, hopes, networks |
| 12th | Rest, retreat, subconscious, endings, compassion |

Planets in a house energize that area; the **sign on the cusp** colors *how* you experience it. House systems (Placidus, Whole Sign, Equal, etc.) can shift cusps—astrologers disclose which system they use.
