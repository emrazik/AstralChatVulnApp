# Understanding Natal Charts

A **natal chart** (also called a birth chart) is a map of where the Sun, Moon, and planets were at the exact moment and location of your birth. Astrologers cast it using date, time, and place; the time fixes the **Ascendant** (rising sign), which rotates roughly every two hours.

The chart is divided into **twelve houses**, each linked to life themes such as identity, resources, communication, home, creativity, work, relationships, transformation, travel, career, community, and the subconscious. **Signs** describe *style*; **houses** describe *where in life* that style shows up.

You can have several planets in one sign, or spread across many. No single placement defines you: astrologers read **patterns**—elements, modalities, aspects between planets, and focal points like a stellium (three or more bodies in one sign or house).

**For Celeste-style readings:** Encourage users who only know their Sun sign to add birth time and city when they can; the Moon and rising sign often explain moods and first impressions the Sun alone misses.
