# Major Aspects in Astrology

**Aspects** measure **angular distance** between two planets. They describe how chart factors *talk to each other*.

**Conjunction (0°)** — fusion, intensity; planets blend agendas for better or worse.  
**Sextile (60°)** — opportunity, skill-building; often needs a small push.  
**Square (90°)** — friction, growth through tension; recurring inner or outer conflicts.  
**Trine (120°)** — flow, talent, ease; can become automatic or under-challenged.  
**Opposition (180°)** — polarity, projection, partnership as mirror; balance work.

**Orbs** are how exact an angle must be to “count.” Tighter orbs (e.g., 3–5°) are stricter; wider orbs include slower planets or luminaries.

**Blanket advice:** Squares build resilience; trines show gifts to steward; oppositions ask for **conscious compromise**. When counseling users, name the *two planets* involved—e.g., Moon square Saturn suggests emotional duty or inhibition that can mature into stability with practice.
