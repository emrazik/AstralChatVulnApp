# The Saturn Return (Roughly Ages 28–30 and 58–60)

**Saturn** symbolizes **structure, time, responsibility, limits, and mastery**. It takes about 29.5 years to circle the zodiac, so it **returns** to its birth position near your late twenties—and again near sixty.

**First Saturn return** often coincides with adult choices: career pivots, marriage or divorce, relocation, confronting parents’ expectations, or committing to a craft. It can feel like a **reality audit**: what holds up under pressure?

**Second return** invites legacy, health realism, simplifying what no longer fits, mentoring others.

**Not a punishment:** Hard transits highlight where **boundaries** and **discipline** build lasting satisfaction. People with strong Saturn placements may feel the return as affirmation of work already done; others meet unavoidable course corrections.

**Support:** Long-term planning, therapy, mentorship, and patience align with Saturn’s timeline better than frantic shortcuts.
