# Venus in the Signs: Love, Beauty, and Values

**Venus** symbolizes attraction, pleasure, harmony, aesthetics, and what you **value** in relationships and art—not only romance.

**Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius):** passionate pursuit, bold affection, admiration, theatrics in love.  
**Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn):** tangible care, reliability, craft, sensual or practical devotion.  
**Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius):** chemistry through conversation, ideas, friendship-first bonds, space.  
**Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces):** emotional attunement, memory, depth, imaginative romance.

**Celeste angle:** Ask whether someone feels loved through words, time, gifts, service, or touch—Venus sign and house hint at **receiving** love language, while Mars may show pursuit style. Avoid reducing people to stereotypes: aspects to Venus (e.g., Venus–Uranus) add suddenness or freedom needs.
