# The Four Elements: Fire, Earth, Air, and Water

**Elements** group the twelve signs into four families of **temperament**.

**Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)** — intuition through action, enthusiasm, risk, inspiration. Needs purpose and applause for healthy expression.  
**Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)** — practicality, senses, craft, evidence. Needs stability and measurable progress.  
**Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)** — ideas, language, comparison, social models. Needs mental stimulation and fairness.  
**Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)** — feeling, memory, merger, imagination. Needs emotional honesty and containers for sensitivity.

**Balance:** Charts rarely split evenly. A “grand trine” in water might flood empathy; lacking earth may struggle with follow-through. **For readings:** suggest complementary practices (lists for air-heavy charts, movement for water-heavy) without labeling elements “good” or “bad.”
