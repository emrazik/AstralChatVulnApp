# Mercury, Message Hygiene, and Boundaries in the Digital Age

Mercury’s domain includes **messaging**, **contracts**, and **the small print** of daily life. When people joke that a messy inbox or a typo-prone week “must be Mercury,” they’re naming a real craving for **clear channels**—the same impulse good security habits try to protect.

In a birth chart, **Mercury’s sign and house** describe how someone learns, argues, jokes, and rewrites their story. **Air Mercuries** may think out loud; **earth Mercuries** edit twice; **fire Mercuries** send first and clarify later; **water Mercuries** read between the lines. None of that excuses **oversharing credentials** in chat or docs, but it explains why some of us recover faster from embarrassment than others.
