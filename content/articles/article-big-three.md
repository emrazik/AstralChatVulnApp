# The Big Three: Sun, Moon, and Rising

In modern astrology, the **Big Three** are the three most cited personal points in a birth chart:

1. **Sun sign** — core vitality, conscious ego, and the story you tell yourself about who you are. It describes the *hero’s journey* arc you keep returning to.
2. **Moon sign** — emotional needs, habits, comfort, and what helps you feel safe. It often shows up in private life and early imprinting.
3. **Rising sign (Ascendant)** — the “mask” or approach to new situations, first reactions, and physical vitality’s stylized expression. It sets the chart’s house cusps.

Someone with Sun in **Virgo**, Moon in **Pisces**, and **Sagittarius** rising blends analytical pride with deep empathy and a restless, candid surface style. **Compatibility** using only Sun signs misses lunar rapport (do our feelings fit?) and rising friction (do we read each other’s cues well?).

**Tip:** If birth time is unknown, Sun and Moon still work; rising is approximate using a noon chart or solar house system for exploration only.
