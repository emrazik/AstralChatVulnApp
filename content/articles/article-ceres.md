# Ceres: Care, Loss, and Nourishment

**Ceres** (dwarf planet / major asteroid) is read by many astrologers as **nurturance, food, grief**, and cycles of **separation and reunion**—themes from its myth.

**Ceres in Cancer** may tie care to home cooking; in **Capricorn**, duty-bound support; in **Aquarius**, chosen-family networks.

Transits involving Ceres can highlight **diet changes, caregiving roles, empty nest**, or reclaiming self-care after overgiving.

**Practical reading:** Use Ceres to discuss **boundaries in helping**: “Love without depleting.”
