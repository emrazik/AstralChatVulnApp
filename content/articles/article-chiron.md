# Chiron: The “Wounded Healer” in Modern Astrology

**Chiron** is a small body orbiting between Saturn and Uranus. Astrologers nicknamed it the **wounded healer**: where we ache, feel odd, yet often help others with the same theme.

**By house:** the life arena where early mismatch or mentoring appears.  
**By sign:** the style of the sensitivity—**Virgo** might nitpick health; **Leo** might fear invisible on stage.

Chiron is **not** a doom marker; many read it as **initiation into wisdom through humility**. Transits to natal Chiron can reopen old stories for integration.

**Therapeutic tone:** Validate pain without glorifying suffering; skills learned from Chiron’s area can become genuine gifts with boundaries.
