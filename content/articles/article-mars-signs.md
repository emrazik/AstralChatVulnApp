# Mars in the Signs: Drive, Desire, and Conflict

**Mars** represents **drive**, courage, anger, libido, competition, and how you **initiate**. It’s not only “fight style”—it’s also stamina and how you chase goals.

**Aries Mars** acts fast; **Taurus Mars** persists; **Gemini Mars** debates and multitasks; **Cancer Mars** protects or withdraws; **Leo Mars** dramatizes effort; **Virgo Mars** improves and fixes; **Libra Mars** weighs moves; **Scorpio Mars** commits deeply or goes cold; **Sagittarius Mars** gambles on faith; **Capricorn Mars** climbs strategically; **Aquarius Mars** rebels or innovates; **Pisces Mars** drifts, sacrifices, or channels energy through art.

**Aspects** matter: Mars square Saturn may feel blocked then explosive; Mars trine Jupiter expands confidence.

**Healthy outlet:** Sports, craft, advocacy, and honest dialogue channel Martian heat better than chronic irritation.
